Lore

Who has worked on what in a GitHub repo, with the PRs behind it

Point Lore at any public GitHub repo

Lore reads the last 12 months of pull requests, reviews, issues and comments, shows who has worked on which parts of the code, and answers questions from that history. Every answer cites the PRs and issues behind it. Public activity shows who worked on something, not who understands it, and Lore says it that way.

Reading the history…

    Areas of the code by how much changed in them, and the people with the most public activity in each. Merged PRs count most, then reviews, then comments; recent work counts more.

    Pick an open issue: Lore finds related past work and who has worked on that part of the code, and drafts a first reply for you to edit. Nothing is posted anywhere, and the draft makes no promises for you or the project.

    Areas with at least 3 merged PRs in the last six months where at most one active person has much public activity. Each row shows the PRs and everyone on them. Few people in an area's history doesn't mean nobody else understands it.

    Reads public GitHub data only, from your browser. Keeps GitHub logins, never emails or names. Answers use your Anna model. Lore 0.1.2
